*AI, quantum, math, and astrophysics: Extract structure from noisy, incomplete data. Map underlying principles: exploit symmetry → extract structure → approximate → reconstruct → know limits → test stability.*

**Trinity of Reduction (How do you make the complicated simple?)**

* **Principle 1**: [Symmetries](https://github.com/deltorobarba/science/blob/main/experiments/symmetries.ipynb) ❄️ What repeats needs computing once: symmetry gives coordinates *a priori*.
* **Principle 2**: [Principal Axes](https://github.com/deltorobarba/science/blob/main/experiments/principal_axes.ipynb) 🧬 No symmetry? Covariance eigenvectors separate signal from noise *a posteriori*.
* **Principle 3**: [Approximation](https://github.com/deltorobarba/science/blob/main/experiments/approximation.ipynb) 🐚 Keep the leading terms and watch where the remainder blows up.

**Duality of Information (What can we know and reconstruct?)**

* **Principle 4**: [Reconstruction](https://github.com/deltorobarba/science/blob/main/experiments/reconstruction.ipynb) 🌸 Get more unknowns than measurements with priors: sparsity, low rank.
* **Principle 5**: [Limits of Knowledge](https://github.com/deltorobarba/science/blob/main/experiments/limits_of_knowledge.ipynb) 🔬 Barriers no algorithm crosses: physical, statistical, computational.

**Dynamics (How does a system evolve?)**

* **Principle 6**: [Stability](https://github.com/deltorobarba/science/blob/main/experiments/stability.ipynb) 🦋 Does small disturbance grow or die out? Sign of one eigenvalue decides.

**Astrophysics**

* [Multiwavelength Astronomy](https://github.com/deltorobarba/science/blob/main/experiments/multiwavelength_astronomy.ipynb) 🔭🪐 Any galaxy or nebula from X-ray to radio, in false color.
* [Galaxies](https://github.com/deltorobarba/science/blob/main/experiments/galaxies.ipynb) 📡 M 51 from UV to infrared, dark matter from rotation curves, the black hole of M 87.
* [Nebulae](https://github.com/deltorobarba/science/blob/main/experiments/nebulae.ipynb) 🛰️ From star birth to supernova: ρ Ophiuchi, Orion, planetary nebulae and the Crab.
* [Solar Astronomy](https://github.com/deltorobarba/science/blob/main/experiments/solar_astronomy.ipynb) ☀️ Sunspot cycles since 1749, 20 000 X-ray flares and strongest flare of cycle 25.
* [Exoplanets](https://github.com/deltorobarba/science/blob/main/experiments/exoplanets.ipynb) 🌗 A Kepler transit search, the radius valley of 6470 planets and a JWST spectrum.
* [Gravitational Waves](https://github.com/deltorobarba/science/blob/main/experiments/gravitational_waves.ipynb) 🌊 GW150914 from raw LIGO data, then mass gaps and selection in the catalog.
* [Cosmology](https://github.com/deltorobarba/science/blob/main/experiments/cosmology.ipynb) 💥 Eight ways to measure the universe, from supernovae to Big Bang nucleosynthesis.
