*Working where AI engineering, quantum computing, mathematics and astrophysics meet. Real problems are organized not by field or tool but by principle: recognize the principle underneath, then pick the tool. The arc: use structure a priori → find it in data → approximate under control → reconstruct what is missing → accept limits → ask whether a disturbance grows.*

**Trinity of Reduction (How do you make the complicated simple?)**

* **Principle 1**: [Symmetries](https://github.com/deltorobarba/science/blob/main/symmetries.ipynb) ❄️ What repeats needs computing only once: symmetry gives the right coordinates *a priori*. Examples: Fourier modes, conserved sectors, Clifford circuits, the Berry phase, Mercury's perihelion, isotropy.
* **Principle 2**: [Principal Axes](https://github.com/deltorobarba/science/blob/main/principal_axes.ipynb) 🧬 Without symmetry, find the relevant directions *a posteriori*: covariance eigenvectors separate signal from noise. Examples: LIGO whitening, Kepler systematics, autoencoders, entangled states, Pantheon+.
* **Principle 3**: [Approximation](https://github.com/deltorobarba/science/blob/main/approximation.ipynb) 🐚 Keep the leading terms or the best member of a family, and watch where the remainder blows up. Examples: radii of convergence, cosmography, post-Newtonian chirps, Trotter error, variational bounds, neural networks outside their training range.

**Duality of Information (What can we know and reconstruct?)**

* **Principle 4**: [Reconstruction from Incomplete Measurements](https://github.com/deltorobarba/science/blob/main/reconstruction.ipynb) 🌸 Recover more unknowns than measurements via a structural prior (sparsity, low rank). Examples: compressed sensing, EHT imaging of M 87\*, quantum tomography, classical shadows.
* **Principle 5**: [Limits of Knowledge](https://github.com/deltorobarba/science/blob/main/limits_of_knowledge.ipynb) 🔬 Barriers no algorithm crosses: physical, statistical, computational. Examples: Rayleigh's curse and SPADE, the Heisenberg limit, learning parity with noise, sizing an AI evaluation.

**Dynamics (How does a system evolve?)**

* **Principle 6**: [Stability](https://github.com/deltorobarba/science/blob/main/stability.ipynb) 🦋 Does a small disturbance grow or die out? The sign of one eigenvalue decides. Examples: Lyapunov exponents, Kirkwood gaps, scrambling and many-body localization, synchronization, neutron freeze-out, feedback and learning rates, optimal control at L2.

**Special Interest Notebooks**

* **Astrophysics**: [Multiwavelength Astronomy](https://github.com/deltorobarba/science/blob/main/multiwavelength_astronomy.ipynb) 🔭🪐 SIMBAD, SkyView and SDSS for any galaxy or nebula: false-color composites from X-ray to radio and emission-line spectra (M 51).
* **Astrophysics**: [Exoplanets](https://github.com/deltorobarba/science/blob/main/exoplanets.ipynb) 🌗 Planets from light, with real data: a Kepler transit search and its vetting, the radius valley of 6470 planets, the JWST spectrum of HATS-6 b, and SPADE below the diffraction limit.
* **Astrophysics**: [Solar Astronomy](https://github.com/deltorobarba/science/blob/main/solar_astronomy.ipynb) ☀️ The solar cycle since 1749, 20 000 GOES X-ray flares, where flares erupt (butterfly diagram), and the strongest flare of cycle 25 in SDO images.
* **Quantum Information**: [Heisenberg-Weyl & Quantum Dynamics](https://github.com/deltorobarba/science/blob/main/heisenbergweyl.ipynb) ⚛️ One recipe, the tensor algebra modulo a degree-2 rule, gives bosons and fermions; up to degree 2 all stays classically simulable. Examples: transmon anharmonicity, qudit clock and shift, Wigner negativity, Clifford+T, Hubbard.
