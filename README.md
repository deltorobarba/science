*Working where AI Engineering, Quantum Computing, Mathematics, and Astrophysics meet. The core challenge is often identical: extracting hidden structure from incomplete, noisy measurements.*

[Reconstruction from Incomplete Measurements](https://github.com/deltorobarba/science/blob/main/reconstruction.ipynb) 🌸

Structural priors ($\ell_1$-sparsity, low rank, positivity) recover underdetermined systems ($m < n$): compressed sensing, EHT imaging of M 87*, quantum tomography, classical shadows, and a Bell state measured on IBM's ibm_aachen.


[Principal Axes](https://github.com/deltorobarba/science/blob/main/principal_axes.ipynb) 🧬

Eigendecomposition of covariance matrices ($C = V\Lambda V^T$) separates signal from noise: LIGO whitening and the GW150914 chirp, Kepler systematics, autoencoders, quantum states, and the Pantheon+ covariance behind H₀.

[Multiwavelength Astronomy](https://github.com/deltorobarba/science/blob/main/multiwavelength_astronomy.ipynb) 🔭🪐

Queries SIMBAD, NASA SkyView and SDSS for any galaxy or nebula: the brightest galaxies, false-color composites from X-ray to radio, and fiber spectra with emission lines in a BPT diagram (M 51).
