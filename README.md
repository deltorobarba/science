*Working where AI Engineering, Quantum Computing, Mathematics, and Astrophysics meet. The core challenge is often identical: extracting hidden structure from incomplete, noisy measurements.*

*Organized by principle, not by field: real problems (simulating a protein, sifting a sky survey) don't come labeled with a method, so the work is to recognize the principle underneath and map the right tool to it. Three principles make the complicated simple: keep the axes that matter, compute what repeats only once, keep the leading terms. Two more ask what can be recovered from incomplete data, and where knowing stops.*

[Principal Axes](https://github.com/deltorobarba/science/blob/main/principal_axes.ipynb) 🧬 Eigendecomposition of covariance matrices ($C = V\Lambda V^T$) separates signal from noise: LIGO whitening and the GW150914 chirp, Kepler systematics, autoencoders, quantum states, and the Pantheon+ covariance behind H₀.

[Symmetries](https://github.com/deltorobarba/science/blob/main/symmetries.ipynb) ❄️ What repeats needs computing only once: Fourier modes, conserved sectors in a spin chain, Clifford circuits on a laptop, the Berry phase, Mercury's perihelion, and what cosmic isotropy tells us (Planck, DESI).

[Approximation](https://github.com/deltorobarba/science/blob/main/approximation.ipynb) 🐚 Keep the leading terms and know the remainder: radii of convergence set by complex poles, Pantheon+ cosmography, post-Newtonian chirps of GW150914, Trotter steps under gate noise, variational bounds from hydrogen to VQE, and neural networks outside their training range.

[Reconstruction from Incomplete Measurements](https://github.com/deltorobarba/science/blob/main/reconstruction.ipynb) 🌸 Structural priors ($\ell_1$-sparsity, low rank, positivity) recover underdetermined systems ($m < n$): compressed sensing, EHT imaging of M 87*, quantum tomography, classical shadows, and a Bell state.

[Limits of Knowledge](https://github.com/deltorobarba/science/blob/main/limits_of_knowledge.ipynb) 🔬 Where physics, statistics, and computation stop us: Rayleigh's curse and quantum-limited SPADE imaging of exoplanets, the Heisenberg limit under noise, learning parity with noise, and how many tasks an AI evaluation needs.


[Multiwavelength Astronomy](https://github.com/deltorobarba/science/blob/main/multiwavelength_astronomy.ipynb) 🔭🪐 Queries SIMBAD, NASA SkyView and SDSS for any galaxy or nebula: the brightest galaxies, false-color composites from X-ray to radio, and fiber spectra with emission lines in a BPT diagram (M 51).
