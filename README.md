*Working where AI engineering, quantum computing, mathematics and astrophysics meet. Real problems are organized not by field or tool but by principle: recognize the principle underneath, then pick the tool. The arc: use structure a priori → find it in data → approximate under control → reconstruct what is missing → accept limits → ask whether a disturbance grows.*

**Trinity of Reduction (How do you make the complicated simple?)**

* **Principle 1**: [Symmetries](https://github.com/deltorobarba/science/blob/main/experiments/symmetries.ipynb) ❄️ What repeats needs computing only once: symmetry gives the right coordinates *a priori*. Examples: Fourier modes, conserved sectors, Clifford circuits, the Berry phase, Mercury's perihelion, isotropy.
* **Principle 2**: [Principal Axes](https://github.com/deltorobarba/science/blob/main/experiments/principal_axes.ipynb) 🧬 Without symmetry, find the relevant directions *a posteriori*: covariance eigenvectors separate signal from noise. Examples: LIGO whitening, Kepler systematics, autoencoders, entangled states, Pantheon+.
* **Principle 3**: [Approximation](https://github.com/deltorobarba/science/blob/main/experiments/approximation.ipynb) 🐚 Keep the leading terms or the best member of a family, and watch where the remainder blows up. Examples: radii of convergence, cosmography, post-Newtonian chirps, Trotter error, variational bounds, neural networks outside their training range.

**Duality of Information (What can we know and reconstruct?)**

* **Principle 4**: [Reconstruction](https://github.com/deltorobarba/science/blob/main/experiments/reconstruction.ipynb) 🌸 (from Incomplete Measurements) Recover more unknowns than measurements via a structural prior (sparsity, low rank). Examples: compressed sensing, EHT imaging of M 87\*, quantum tomography, classical shadows.
* **Principle 5**: [Limits of Knowledge](https://github.com/deltorobarba/science/blob/main/experiments/limits_of_knowledge.ipynb) 🔬 Barriers no algorithm crosses: physical, statistical, computational. Examples: Rayleigh's curse and SPADE, the Heisenberg limit, learning parity with noise, sizing an AI evaluation.

**Dynamics (How does a system evolve?)**

* **Principle 6**: [Stability](https://github.com/deltorobarba/science/blob/main/experiments/stability.ipynb) 🦋 Does a small disturbance grow or die out? The sign of one eigenvalue decides. Examples: Lyapunov exponents, Kirkwood gaps, scrambling and many-body localization, synchronization, neutron freeze-out, feedback and learning rates, optimal control at L2.

**Astrophysics**

* [Multiwavelength Astronomy](https://github.com/deltorobarba/science/blob/main/experiments/multiwavelength_astronomy.ipynb) 🔭🪐 SIMBAD, SkyView and SDSS for any galaxy or nebula: false-color composites from X-ray to radio and emission-line spectra (M 51).
* [Galaxies](https://github.com/deltorobarba/science/blob/main/experiments/galaxies.ipynb) 📡 One galaxy from UV to infrared (M 51), gas and oxygen in M 101, dark matter from 175 rotation curves, warped disks seen edge-on, and the black hole of M 87 from Event Horizon Telescope data.
* [Nebulae](https://github.com/deltorobarba/science/blob/main/experiments/nebulae.ipynb) 🛰️ Five nebulae from star birth to supernova: the dark cloud ρ Ophiuchi, Orion from X-ray to radio, gas temperatures in M 101, planetary nebulae out to M 31, and the Crab expanding since 1054.
* [Solar Astronomy](https://github.com/deltorobarba/science/blob/main/experiments/solar_astronomy.ipynb) ☀️ The solar cycle since 1749, 20 000 GOES X-ray flares, where flares erupt (butterfly diagram), and the strongest flare of cycle 25 in SDO images.
* [Exoplanets](https://github.com/deltorobarba/science/blob/main/experiments/exoplanets.ipynb) 🌗 Planets from light, with real data: a Kepler transit search and its vetting, the radius valley of 6470 planets, the JWST spectrum of HATS-6 b, and SPADE below the diffraction limit.
* [Gravitational Waves](https://github.com/deltorobarba/science/blob/main/experiments/gravitational_waves.ipynb) 🌊 GW150914 from raw LIGO data to its chirp mass, then GWOSC catalog: mass gaps, detector reach growing as M^(5/6), and the selection effect behind the black-hole mass distribution.
* [Cosmology](https://github.com/deltorobarba/science/blob/main/experiments/cosmology.ipynb) 💥 Eight ways to measure the universe, from today back to the first minutes: supernovae, a local void, an N-body simulation, the siren GW170817, cosmic clocks, DESI BAO, Planck's acoustic peaks, and Big Bang nucleosynthesis.

**Mathematics & Quantum**

* [Heisenberg-Weyl](https://github.com/deltorobarba/science/blob/main/experiments/heisenbergweyl.ipynb) ⚛️ One recipe, the tensor algebra modulo a degree-2 rule, gives bosons and fermions; up to degree 2 all stays classically simulable. Examples: transmon anharmonicity, qudit clock and shift, Wigner negativity, Clifford+T, Hubbard.
* [Bell State Preparation](https://github.com/deltorobarba/science/blob/main/experiments/bellstatepreparation.ipynb) $\vert\Phi^+\rangle$ 🔗 From two abstract gates to noisy quantum hardware: build $\vert{}\Phi^+\rangle$, transpile to native Heron gates, and compare ideal simulation, calibrated noise, and real IBM QPU runs. Covers: Hadamard & CNOT, entanglement, transpilation ($R_z$, $\sqrt{X}$, CZ), $T_1$/$T_2$ decoherence, and readout errors.
* [Quantum Information](https://github.com/deltorobarba/science/blob/main/experiments/quantum_information.ipynb) 🎲 From the Born rule to channel capacities: POVMs, weak and continuous measurement, the Zeno effect, tomography and classical shadows, then distances, entropies, the Holevo bound, no-cloning and quantum Fisher information.
* [Algebra](https://github.com/deltorobarba/science/blob/main/experiments/algebra.ipynb) 🧮 Groups, rings and fields, eigenvalues and Lie theory as one ladder: from Cayley tables, Rubik's cube and the Monster to finite fields, Reed-Solomon codes, the QR algorithm, quantum phase estimation, root systems and SU(3).
* [Dynamics](https://github.com/deltorobarba/science/blob/main/experiments/dynamics.ipynb) 🌪️ Where equations of motion come from, how to solve them and when they turn unpredictable: the brachistochrone and Noether, integrators, stiffness and CFL, tunneling, Floquet, the logistic map and the Lorenz attractor.
* [Geometry](https://github.com/deltorobarba/science/blob/main/experiments/geometry.ipynb) 📐 What stays invariant: vector calculus from Green and Stokes to quantized vortices, curvature from the sphere to light bending, projective geometry from vanishing points to the Bloch sphere, and elliptic curves up to Shor's algorithm.
