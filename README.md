*Working where AI Engineering, Quantum Computing, Mathematics, and Astrophysics meet. The core challenge is often identical: extracting hidden structure from incomplete, noisy measurements. These notebooks and Python modules implement the underlying mathematical bridges—from random matrix theory and $\ell_1$-regularization to quantum hardware execution—on real-world datasets.*

[Reconstruction from Incomplete Measurements](https://github.com/deltorobarba/science/blob/main/reconstruction.ipynb) 🌸

Explores how structural priors ($\ell_1$-sparsity and low matrix rank) allow exact recovery of underdetermined systems ($m < n$) across three disciplines. Connects mathematical phase transitions in Compressed Sensing to interferometric black hole imaging of M 87* (EHT), low-rank Quantum State Tomography / Classical Shadows, and quantum simulation of the entangled Bell state $\vert{}\Phi^+\rangle$ with native gate transpilation ($R_z, \sqrt{X}, \text{CZ}$).


[Principal Axes](https://github.com/deltorobarba/science/blob/main/principal_axes.ipynb) 🧬

Demonstrates how eigendecomposition of covariance matrices ($C = V\Lambda V^T$) separates true signal from random noise (Marchenko-Pastur / BBP threshold) across four domains. Unifies spectral whitening of LIGO gravitational wave data (GW150914), systematic detrending of Kepler light curves, linear Autoencoders in ML, and Schmidt decomposition of entangled quantum states.

[Multiwavelength Astronomy](https://github.com/deltorobarba/science/blob/main/multiwavelength_astronomy.ipynb) 🔭🪐

Automated pipeline querying SIMBAD und NASA SkyView to map Messier objects and render multi-band false-color composites from X-ray to radio. Reveals how different physical components—such as cold dust, old stellar populations, and young star-forming regions in M 51—dominate across the electromagnetic spectrum.
